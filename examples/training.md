# Worked example — Training / explainer

Request: "Make a 10-slide training deck that explains our new expense policy to staff. Keep it practical."

Structure used: 3. Training / explainer.

---

## Slide 1 — The new policy removes pre-approval for anything under $200

- Effective 1 October
- Pre-approval still required above $200
- Receipts required for everything

Visual: single sentence on a plain background

Notes: "One change matters most: under two hundred dollars, you no longer need approval before you spend."

## Slide 2 — Why we are changing it

- Median approval wait was 3.2 days
- 61% of requests were under $200
- Finance spent a third of its week on low-value approvals

Visual: bar chart of request volume by amount band

Notes: "Most requests were small and slow. We are removing the slow part, not the control."

## Slide 3 — Three things that are not changing

- Receipts are still mandatory
- Monthly submission deadline is still the 5th
- Client entertainment still needs a named attendee list

Visual: three-item list with a lock icon

Notes: "If you remember nothing else, remember the receipts."

## Slide 4 — What you do now, step by step

- Buy, then photograph the receipt
- Upload it in the expense app within 7 days
- Categorise and submit

Visual: three-step flow with app screenshots

Notes: "The only new habit is the seven-day upload window. Everything else is the same flow."

## Slide 5 — A worked example

- Lunch with a client, $86
- No pre-approval needed
- Receipt photographed, uploaded, categorised as Client Meals
- Reimbursed in the next cycle

Visual: annotated app screen showing each step

Notes: "This is the most common case, and it now takes about forty seconds."

## Slide 6 — Do and do not

- Do upload within 7 days
- Do keep the original receipt for 90 days
- Do not split a purchase to stay under $200
- Do not submit after the 5th and expect same-cycle payment

Visual: two-column do / do not table

Notes: "Splitting a purchase is the one that gets escalated. Please do not."

## Slide 7 — Practice scenario

- You need a $650 conference ticket
- What do you do first?

Visual: scenario text with a blank answer space

Notes: "Talk to the person next to you for thirty seconds. The answer is pre-approval, before purchase."

## Slide 8 — Recap

- Under $200: no pre-approval
- Over $200: pre-approval before purchase
- Receipts always, upload within 7 days
- Submit by the 5th

Visual: four-item summary card

Notes: "Four rules. If you follow them, nothing changes for you except the approval wait."

## Slide 9 — Where to get help

- Expense app help centre
- Finance channel for policy questions
- Your manager for exceptions

Visual: three links with a QR code

Notes: "Questions go to the finance channel, not to individual emails. That way everyone sees the answer."

## Slide 10 — Questions

- Anything unclear
- Edge cases you have hit

Visual: none

Notes: "I will stay after the session for anything specific to your team."
