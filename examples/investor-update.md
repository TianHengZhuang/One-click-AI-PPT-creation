# Worked example — Investor update

Request: "Write the monthly investor update for a seed-stage company. 7 slides, direct tone."

Structure used: 11. Investor update.

---

## Slide 1 — We hit 1,100 paying teams and cut burn by 15%

- Net new: 140 teams, the best month since launch
- Burn down to $118K/month
- Runway: 21 months

Visual: two-line chart, teams and burn, on the same months

Notes: "Two things matter this month: growth held and burn came down. Those two lines are the whole update."

## Slide 2 — Metrics

- ARR $1.9M, up 11% month over month
- Net revenue retention 108%
- Logo churn 1.9%, down from 2.4%

Visual: metrics table with the same rows as every previous update

Notes: "Same table as always, so you can read the trend, not just the value."

## Slide 3 — What worked

- Self-serve onboarding now converts 24% of trials, up from 17%
- The API launch brought 60 teams in without sales involvement
- Support tickets per account down 18% after the docs rewrite

Visual: funnel diagram with the conversion change highlighted

Notes: "The onboarding change is the single highest-leverage thing we did this month."

## Slide 4 — What did not work

- Enterprise pilot with Acme stalled at security review
- Our pricing page test produced no measurable difference
- Hiring for the second AE took six weeks longer than planned

Visual: three-item list with the response next to each

Notes: "The Acme stall is process, not product. The pricing test tells us the page is not the constraint."

## Slide 5 — Asks

- Two introductions to mid-market finance leads
- A referral for a security-compliance contractor
- Feedback on the enterprise pricing draft

Visual: three ask cards, each with a one-line context

Notes: "The compliance contractor is the one that unblocks Acme and the next two pilots."

## Slide 6 — Cash and runway

- $2.5M in the bank
- Burn $118K/month, down 15%
- Default plan reaches 24 months of runway

Visual: cash curve with the current plan and the conservative plan

Notes: "We are not raising in the next two quarters. This curve is why."

## Slide 7 — Next milestone and date

- Target: 1,500 paying teams by 31 December
- Second AE starts 6 October
- Enterprise tier pricing decision by 20 October

Visual: single timeline with the three dates

Notes: "If we miss 1,500, the reason will be enterprise, not self-serve. That is where I am spending my time."
